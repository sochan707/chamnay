# Timeline Definition Backlog — Budget Planner

**Purpose:** Set dates for [Milestone 1](milestone_1.md), then replace the illustrative six-week plan in [business_case_draft.md](business_case_draft.md) with a timeline the group can commit to.  
**Current status:** Six weeks is an assumption. The submission date, team availability, and MVP estimates are unconfirmed. The group has decided not to run a survey.  
**Working method:** Complete one item per short work session (about 12 minutes), then record the result or the blocker. Assign one owner to each item when the group meets.

## Prioritized backlog

| Priority | Backlog item | What to do | Done when | Depends on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm the fixed dates | Record the Milestone 1 due date, course submission deadline, earliest start date, holidays, review dates, and any sponsor or class checkpoints. | Dates and their sources are written below; unknown dates are marked `TBD`. | Assignment brief / group |
| 2 | Confirm team capacity | Record each person's available hours per week and weeks they cannot work. Name an owner for research, design, build, and testing. | A week-by-week capacity total is available; unconfirmed availability is marked `TBD`. | Group members |
| 3 | Decide the delivery scope | Rank the four MVP capabilities in the business case against the project goal and available time. Identify anything that can be deferred. | There is a must-have list and a deferred list approved by the group. | Business case section 3 |
| 4 | Break work into deliverables | List research, design, setup, each MVP capability, integration, usability testing, fixes, pilot, and final report. | Each deliverable has an observable output and one owner. | Scope decision |
| 5 | Estimate effort and dependencies | Estimate hours for each deliverable with the people doing the work. Mark tasks that must finish before others start. | Estimates fit the capacity table or the scope is reduced; the dependency chain is visible. | Items 2 and 4 |
| 6 | Schedule milestones | Place discovery decision, prototype review, feature completion, test-ready build, pilot start, and final review before the fixed deadline. | Every milestone has a date, owner, and completion criterion. | Items 1 and 5 |
| 7 | Add review and contingency time | Reserve time for feedback, defect fixes, and integration. Decide what to cut if a milestone slips. | The plan has a visible buffer and a named fallback for the main schedule risks. | Item 6 |
| 8 | Validate and publish the timeline | Walk through the plan with the group; update the delivery plan and matching budget assumptions in the business case. | The group agrees that dates, scope, hours, and budget tell the same story; remaining assumptions are labeled. | Items 1–7 |
